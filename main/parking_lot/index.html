This library provides compact and efficient implementations of Mutex, FairMutex, ReentrantMutex, RwLock, RecursiveRwLock, Condvar and Once.

§Use in global allocators

The synchronization primitives in this crate must not be used internally by a global allocator. Their contended paths may allocate memory, including while internal parking-lot locks are held. If the allocator then blocks on one of these primitives, it may recursively invoke itself or deadlock.
